How you move through the house

  1. Enter the 14'7" main gate on the east, or the 4'11" side gate on the west.
  2. Cross the parking to the porch on the east. Climb 3 steps, go through the 5' double main door into the foyer.
  3. The foyer opens into the living room. Bedroom 1 opens off the living room, with the study and toilet 1 inside it.
  4. A 5' opening leads from the living room to the passage beside the kitchen, with the fridge and shoe cabinet on the west wall.
  5. The passage reaches the stair hall. From there you get to common toilet 2, bedroom 2, the door to the garden and the spiral stair.
  6. The spiral stair rises to the first-floor landing. From there you reach bedroom 3 and its toilet, or step out onto the open terrace.

Staircase 1, the outside stair

  • Flight A runs along the west wall from 4' inside the road to the house. It is 3'6" wide and 12' long and rises 0 to 7'9" with 6" risers and 10" treads.
  • A 3'6" square landing at 7'9" sits beside the west wall.
  • Flight B turns east, runs 5'6" long and rises 7'9" to 11'9½", then arrives on balcony 2. The car parks under flight B and the landing.

Heights

  • Floor to floor: 11'9½". Wall height: 10'2½". The first-floor slab extends 11'6" past the house front over the parking.

Points to confirm

  1. Neem tree and car. With the trunk 7'6" from the west boundary and 3'6" from the road, it stands right in front of the car's spot. The gap between the trunk and Staircase 1 is about 3'10", and the car is 6' wide. Is the trunk position right, or does the car come in on the east side and shift over?
  2. Staircase 1 risers. 7'9" is 15½ risers of 6", and the full 11'9½" is 23½. The landing height needs a small adjustment, for example 24 risers of about 5.9".
  3. Windows. Only two windows are placed: bedroom 2's back window and the study's front window. The living room, kitchen, toilets, bedroom 1's main wall and bedroom 3 have none yet. The east side can take windows over the 2'3" passage. The west side sits on the boundary.
  4. First-floor overhangs. The bedroom 3 block projects 2'4" past bedroom 2 into the garden. The slab over the parking projects 11'6", which needs columns or a beam at its front edge.
  5. Toilet 2 door opens outward into the stair hall. Bedroom 2's door swings in the same area.
