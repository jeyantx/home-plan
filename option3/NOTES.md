Walls: outer 9", partitions 6". Floor-to-floor: ground 10'6", hall 11'.

## What is kept from your plan
- Bedroom 1 at the front beside the entry, with an L-shaped living wrapping it.
- Kitchen behind the living, then an open-to-sky garden, then bedroom 2 behind the garden.
- Bedroom 3 above bedroom 2, built in phase 1 with the internal stair.
- A separate external stair for the hall.

## What changed and why
- Parking shortened from 35' to 24'. The slab above starts at 14', clear of the neem tree, so the car and bikes are covered.
- External stair moved from the tree side (west, 7'6" from the trunk) to run along the west wall behind the tree, starting at 9'6". Risers 7" not 6", so it fits in 14'.
- Internal stair widened from 2'9" to two flights of 3'3" and moved to the west wall so it lands outside the hall. No toilet under it. A 2' cupboard wall faces the dining instead.
- Toilet 1 is now common and sits between bedroom 1 and the living, so parents and guests share it without walking.
- Garden grown from 119 sqft to 176 sqft open, reached from the kitchen door and from the dining through the covered walk. Utility with washing machine sits beside the kitchen door.
- Bedroom 2 grown to 155 sqft with the big bath. Bedroom 3 is the same size above it. Their toilets stack, one plumbing shaft.
- A 172 sqft rear yard behind bedroom 2 for the borewell, clothes line and rain pit.
- No passage anywhere except the 3'3" covered walk along the garden, which doubles as the bridge support.

## First floor

- Phase 1: bedroom 3 wing over bedroom 2, 155 sqft with toilet 3 and lobby. Internal stair arrives at the back-west corner of the house, a 3'3" covered bridge over the garden walk leads to the wing. Overhead tank on this wing's roof at about 21'.
- Phase 1 also casts the slab from 14' to 68'6" as the hall floor, designed for assembly load (4 kN/m2).
- Phase 2: lobby 11'3" x 19' over the portico with 2 toilets (pipes drop in a duct at the portico), hall 41' x 19' = 779 sqft gross, 714 sqft clear of the 10' x 6'6" stair box. The stair box has a lockable door to the hall and a door onto the bridge, so renting the hall never gives access to bedroom 3.

## Structure for the two-phase build
- 6 RCC columns per side in the side walls at x = 14, 24, 37'3", 47, 56'6", 67'6". Beams span the full 19'. Hall is column-free.
- Footings and columns sized for ground + hall + hall roof from day one. Leave dowels at the roof after phase 1 and waterproof it.
- Drill the borewell before construction starts, since the rig cannot reach the rear yard later.
