How to read this plan

  • X is feet from the east boundary, from 0 to 21. Y is feet from the road, from 0 to 106. For example, "GW-05 at Y 50'" names one exact spot.
  • Codes: G- ground-floor room or area, F- first-floor room or area, GW- ground-floor wall, FW- first-floor wall, PW- parapet, BW- boundary wall, D- door, W- window, V- ventilator, O- open arch, GT- gate, ST- staircase.
  • Room sizes are clear inside the walls. Walls are drawn at true thickness: 9" outer and 4½" partitions, compound walls and parapets.
  • Use the checkboxes above the plan to hide codes or dimensions when you want a clean view.

  1. All building walls are squared to a right-angle grid. The plot boundary stays as it is, with the 1' step and the sloping back wall.
  2. The house is 18'3" wide outside and 16'9" inside, leaving a 2'4½" side passage on the east. Your living room width moves from 16'7" to 16'9".
  3. Toilet 1 is now 6'1½" x 4'6", up from 6' x 4'.
  4. Floor to floor is set at 11'6", down from 11'9½", so staircase 1 gets whole 6" risers: 15 to the landing and 8 more to the balcony.
  5. Door sizes are set: main door 4' double, room doors 3', toilet doors 2'6". Both toilet doors open inward; toilet 2's used to open into the stair hall.
  6. D-05 is now a real door to the side garden instead of an open frame.
  7. I added 11 windows and 2 ventilators, on the east, front and back walls only. The west wall sits on the boundary, so it has no openings.
  8. Bedroom 1 had no outside wall, so it gets W-02 facing the porch, plus the study window W-01 to the front.
  9. Gates: a 14'7" main sliding gate GT-01 and a 4'6" side gate GT-02 that leads straight to staircase 1, on three 9" pillars.

Decisions for your approval
