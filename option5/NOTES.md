# Option 5: stair outside the hall, all sizes inside wall faces

Fixes over Option 4:
1. The internal stair sits in its own 7'3" band behind the hall's back wall. The hall is a clean rectangle with nothing inside it.
2. The hall toilets moved to the east side of the lobby. The external stair arrives on the west side. No overlap.
3. Every room size below is measured between finished wall faces, computed from the drawing coordinates.

Inside width: 16'9" at the front, 15'9" at the back (21' plot, 6" compound wall, 1'3" + 1' setbacks, two 9" walls).
