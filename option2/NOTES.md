## First floor (phase 2)

- Slab from 14' to 70' is built in phase 1 as the ground-floor roof. Design it for assembly live load (4 kN/m2), not residential load.
- 14' to 27'3": open lobby and balcony, 13' x 19'. External stair arrives at the west corner. Two toilets 6' x 5' and 6' x 4'6" at the west side, pipes drop in a duct at the portico.
- 27'3" to 70': hall 41'3" x 18'11" inside. 780 sqft gross, 715 sqft clear of the stair box. Rectangular, no pillars. Beams span the 19' width at the 6 column lines. High windows on both sides, sill 6', above the neighbour walls.
- Internal stair arrives in a 10' x 6'6" box on the east wall with a lockable door, so the hall can be rented without access to the house. The same stair continues to the hall roof in phase 3.
- 70' to 78': void over the garden well, with a 3' bridge on the west to the terrace.
- 78' to 101'6": open terrace on the roof of the bedroom wing, for clothes drying.
- Overhead tank on the stair-box roof (about 22' high) so it has head for the first-floor toilets. In phase 1 it sits on the stair headroom slab at the same spot.

## Structure for the two-phase build

- 6 RCC columns per side in the side walls at x = 14, 24, 36'9", 47'3", 58'3", 69'3". Footings sized for ground + hall + hall roof.
- Beams across the full 19' at each column line. Hall is column-free.
- Leave column dowels and a waterproofed roof after phase 1. The bedroom wing (78' to 101'6") is single storey with its own footings.
- Two exits from the hall: the external stair and the internal stair.
