How to read this plan

  • X is feet from the east boundary, from 0 to 21. Y is feet from the road, from 0 to 106. For example, "GW-05 at Y 50'" names one exact spot.
  • Codes: G- ground-floor room or area, F- first-floor room or area, GW- ground-floor wall, FW- first-floor wall, PW- parapet, BW- boundary wall, D- door, W- window, V- ventilator, O- open arch, GT- gate, ST- staircase, UG- underground tank or pit.
  • Room sizes are clear inside the walls. Walls are drawn at true thickness: 9" outer and 4½" partitions, compound walls and parapets.
  • Use Show above the plan to hide codes or dimensions when you want a clean view.
